# SiteIntel Pulse — Product Specification Baseline (Milestone 1)

## Product Identity
- **Product Name:** SiteIntel Pulse — Website Intelligence & Growth Audit Platform
- **Tagline:** Discover Issues. Prove with Evidence. Improve. Grow.
- **Milestone Scope:** Milestone 1 — Complete Website Owner Audit Platform (Company A Report)

## Core Mission
SiteIntel Pulse enables website owners (Company A) to assess technical health, search visibility, performance, mobile experience, public security posture, observable authentication, content quality, AI readiness, and analytics indications in business terms. It translates complex technical measurements into a clear, evidence-based roadmap for platform improvement and growth.

## Audiences & Two-Milestone Roadmap
1. **Company A (Milestone 1 — Complete):** Decision-makers, business owners, and non-technical stakeholders who need to understand website strengths, major weaknesses, competitive context, and actionable business opportunities without getting lost in code-level minutiae.
2. **Company B (Milestone 2 — Documented Architecture & Preview):** Technical agencies, developers, SEO specialists, and security teams who need exact DOM selectors, stack traces, request logs, authenticated auditing, and code fix instructions.

## Report Delivery Architecture
- Public scan entry via domain or HTTPS URL.
- Controlled crawl with SSRF and resource protection.
- 13 distinct report sections rendered on web and exported to 6–12 page professional PDF.
