# SiteIntel Pulse — Permanent Business Model, Monetization & Marketing Charter

**Status:** Permanent Architectural & Strategic Requirement  
**Effective Date:** 1 November 2026  
**Scope:** Universal (Enforced across all present and future phases)

---

## 1. Core Principle: 100% Free Platform for Everyone

SiteIntel Pulse is, and will permanently remain, completely free for all website owners, developers, technical consultants, agencies, and businesses.

### Strictly Prohibited Across All Current & Future Phases:
- **No Subscriptions:** No recurring monthly, quarterly, or annual billing.
- **No Premium or "Pro" Plans:** No tier-gated features or artificial feature crippling.
- **No Paywalls:** No barriers preventing access to full findings, reports, or data.
- **No Paid Memberships:** No member-only diagnostic areas or gated tools.
- **No Credit-Based Access:** No token systems, scan quotas for purchase, or metered scans.
- **No Paid Report Unlocking:** Full Owner Reports, Expert Diagnostic Workbenches, PDF downloads, and CSV rule ledgers are 100% free.
- **No Billing Integrations:** Absolute ban on subscription billing gateways (e.g., Stripe subscriptions, Paddle, Recurly) designed to restrict access.

Every feature built into SiteIntel Pulse—from the 56-rule audit catalogue and Core Web Vitals lab benchmarks to multi-tenant workspaces, remediation task tracking, and cryptographic audit manifests—is accessible without financial charge.

## 2. Monetization Strategy: Ethical Advertising via Google AdSense

The platform's business model is sustained through advertising revenue, with Google AdSense as the primary monetization partner.

### Primary Objectives:
1. **Drive Genuine Organic Traffic:** Attract high-intent website owners, developers, and consultants searching for actionable website diagnostics and technical SEO guidance.
2. **Build Audience Trust & Authority:** Deliver evidence-grounded reports that users trust, share, and return to.
3. **Publish Valuable Original Content:** Build a deep, authoritative library of educational articles, benchmarks, and technical guides.
4. **Foster User Engagement & Retention:** Provide continuous value through remediation tracking, historical audit comparisons, and ongoing health monitoring.
5. **Establish Sustainable Search Visibility:** Rank organically through technical excellence, helpful people-first content, and comprehensive structured data.
6. **Achieve Google AdSense Compliance:** Structure public pages and editorial content to meet and exceed all Google Publisher Policies and Program Policies.
7. **Monetize Without User Friction:** Generate sustainable advertising revenue on appropriate public editorial and educational pages without placing ads inside private dashboards, report workbenches, or utilities.

*Note: In accordance with our principles, SiteIntel Pulse never guarantees AdSense approval, specific search rankings, AI citations, or traffic outcomes.*

## 3. Comprehensive Marketing & Discovery Ecosystem

To achieve sustainable organic audience acquisition, SiteIntel Pulse maintains an end-to-end discovery engine spanning:

- **Technical SEO:** Flawless crawlability, semantic HTML5, zero render-blocking bottlenecks, canonical tags, automated XML sitemaps, and strict `robots.txt` governance.
- **On-Page & Off-Page SEO:** Exhaustive keyword-to-page mapping, semantic heading outlines, descriptive metadata, and natural, earned authority.
- **Google Search Central Standards:** Full adherence to Google Search Essentials, Core Web Vitals lab and field performance baselines, and structured data standards (`Organization`, `WebSite`, `WebPage`, `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`).
- **Webmaster Platform Integration:** Active verification, diagnostic monitoring, and sitemap processing via Google Search Console and Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools.
- **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO):** Direct, verifiable answers, structured data tables, and evidence-grounded definitions designed for discoverability across ChatGPT Search, Microsoft Copilot, Claude Search, and Perplexity AI.
- **Bot Policy Governance:** Explicit user-agent policies in `robots.txt` distinguishing search/retrieval crawlers (permitted), user-initiated fetch agents (permitted), and model-training scrapers (configurable).
- **Internal Content Clusters:** Well-structured topic pillars (Technical SEO, Core Web Vitals, Mobile Responsiveness, Public Security Hygiene, GEO/AEO Readiness) linked via crawlable, contextual navigation.

## 4. Content & Engagement Infrastructure

High-quality, human-reviewed, and verifiable editorial publishing drives platform authority and AdSense eligibility:

- **Editorial Standards:** Every article, guide, and product update must provide original analysis, verified facts, and actionable insights.
- **Strictly Prohibited Content Practices:**
  - No mass-published, low-value AI generated filler.
  - No scraping, spinning, or rewriting competitor articles.
  - No search-engine-first doorway pages or thin programmatic spam.
- **Publishing Hub Components:**
  - Fast, accessible `/blog/` index with structured category pillars.
  - Detailed `/blog/[slug]/` article templates featuring author attribution, publication/update timestamps, and structured JSON-LD.
  - Contextual, non-intrusive CTAs to run free website audits.
  - FAQ and knowledge-base sections addressing genuine technical questions.
  - Valid RSS/Atom feed (`/feed.xml`) for syndication.

## 5. Google & AdSense Policy Compliance

All advertising and content implementations must strictly adhere to current Google standards:

- **Authoritative Sources Consulted:**
  - Google Search Central Documentation.
  - Google Search Console Help Center.
  - Google AdSense Program Policies.
  - Google Publisher Policies.
  - Schema.org & Google Structured Data Guidelines.
- **Uncompromising Compliance Standards:**
  - **Zero Fake Traffic:** Strict prohibition of traffic exchanges, paid-to-click networks, automated traffic bots, or artificially inflated metrics.
  - **Zero Deceptive Placements:** Ads must never mimic navigation buttons, download links, audit action buttons, or report findings.
  - **Clean Utility Surfaces:** No ads on login screens, scan intake queues, private report workbenches, or API responses. Initial ad placements are restricted to high-quality, content-rich public editorial pages.
  - **Core Web Vitals Protection:** Ad containers must use reserved sizing to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) and adhere to FID/INP performance thresholds.
  - **Consent Management:** Compliant consent collection (Google-certified CMP / IAB TCF framework) when serving ads in regulated jurisdictions (EEA, UK, Switzerland).
